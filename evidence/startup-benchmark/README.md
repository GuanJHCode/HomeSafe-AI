# Startup benchmark evidence archive

This selected archive preserves the completed PR-1 overhead experiment, PR-2's preregistration review and its subsequently completed fixed optimization comparison. Interpretation and limits are in [the independent review](../REVIEW_STARTUP_BENCHMARK_20261104.md). It contains no private asset or runtime binary.

`overhead-result.json` is the complete operator-produced result, copied without alteration. `overhead-registration.json`, `paired-startup.mjs`, `paired-startup.test.mjs`, `measurement-fingerprints.json` and `measurement/` preserve the exact frozen measurement code and its relative imports. `A-source-tree.txt` and `B-source-runtime-inventory.json` document baseline sources; `PR2-candidate-source-runtime-inventory.json` belongs only to the separate optimization registration. Independent review receipts name the checks actually performed.

The pre-run audit JSON was serialized after the reviewer had already sent the parent permission to proceed. Its `reviewed_at` records that file-writing time, which falls after the recorded experiment start; the independent review explains this chronology without changing the historical field or inventing a timestamp for the approval message.

These are archived records, not an instruction to replay the completed experiment. The original directory `/workspace/scratch/startup-benchmark` retains the completed registration's exclusive `.run-claim`. Reproduction would require separately approved scheduling/provenance, matching frozen distributions at the registered URLs, the recorded external browser/driver paths and a separately identified registration. The archived directory does not include those distributions or rebuild all source trees. Do not alter the old registration or overwrite its result to seek a favorable sample.

Only `instrumentation_overhead` was measured in `overhead-result.json`; its optimization gate is NOT_APPLICABLE. The later `optimization-result.json` is a separate fixed round: five improving pairs and median savings 334.4 ms meet PR-2's at-least-four-pairs/100-ms signal, subject to the separate correctness review. Candidate first-editable p95 3877.9 ms still fails the 3000 ms budget. Hot evaluation and final full enforce are separate checks. Original result/registration/helper files remain unchanged; new result/audit files were added.
